About Us

Who We Are

DRK Ventures Trading and Services W.L.L. is a manpower supply and recruitment company based in Doha, Qatar. We specialize in connecting businesses across Qatar with qualified, reliable staff for a wide range of roles — from wholesale and office work to hospitality, cleaning, and driving.

We are a licensed commercial entity operating under Qatari regulations, committed to supplying dependable manpower that helps your business run smoothly. Our mission is simple: deliver the right people, on time, every time.

We believe in reliability, speed, compliance, and building long-term partnerships with every client we serve.
